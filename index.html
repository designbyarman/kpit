The Editorial Charter

Dignity Before Dramatic Effect

Keep It Real was founded on an elementary thesis: India does not lack courage; it suffers an epidemic of superficial storytelling.

The Breaking Point (2019)

In the monsoon of 2018, director Kabir Varma spent three weeks filming a flood rescue operation in rural Assam for a prominent network. During editing, network executives demanded dramatic orchestral strings, manipulated audio screams, and a villain narrative.

The villagers who had rowed banana-stem rafts for 72 hours straight were treated as decorative victims rather than masters of engineering and courage. Kabir resigned the following Monday.

The Unwritten Rule

Every Keep It Real film begins with tea, unrecorded conversations, and the subject's explicit consent on how their craft or struggle will be presented to the world.

We never pay people to cry. We never tell an artisan where to sit for dramatic sunlight. If natural reality is slow, our editing respects the rhythm of their hands.

"Sensationalism is the easiest shortcut in media. Reverence is the hardest craft in documentary cinema."
Kabir Varma — Founder & Directing Cinematographer

Operational Clarifications

We operate with a strict separation between funding and story truth. Partners co-determine the high-level impact theme and protagonist community, but our directing team maintains final cut on pacing, authentic dialogue, and natural framing. No scripted corporate speeches or staged testimonials are ever permitted.
An authentic documentary cannot be rushed. Our typical lifecycle spans 8 to 12 weeks: 3 weeks for ground research and ethics consent, 10 days of on-location cinematography, followed by 4–5 weeks of editing, sound design, regional translations, and impact rollouts.
Protagonist dignity is sacred. We conduct vernacular field visits, ensure informed non-coercive consent in their native tongue, verify life facts with local community leaders, and guarantee that their security, dignity, and cultural sensitivities remain unthreatened by global public broadcast.
We never take an administrative fee or platform cut from money given by our viewers. For each subject, we set up verified zero-fee direct accounts or audited community trust escrows, and share bank receipts in quarterly update videos.
